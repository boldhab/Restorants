# Phase 3 transaction rules

Guest checkout is enabled through a server-issued, signed HttpOnly, tenant-scoped credential, stored hashed in PostgreSQL, expiring after 30 days. Mutation requests also require a session-bound guest CSRF header. Account sessions retain existing CSRF protection. Cart IDs and safe order references are never credentials. Login preserves the browser's guest cart and revalidates it; orders placed while authenticated belong to that account. Email matching never claims guest history. Authenticated carts/history are inaccessible after logout; guest history remains limited to the browser's tenant credential.

Pickup is the only fulfillment mode. Delivery, scheduled orders and dine-in ordering are disabled. Ordering and reservations default to disabled in business settings and default basic plan entitlements; platform administrators may manually assign the pilot plan. Owners must independently enable each feature. Immediate pickup requires restaurant/default branch open now and at the configured preparation lead time (default 15 minutes). Default minimum is zero. Restaurant staff explicitly accept orders. Orders expire after 30 minutes pending acceptance; prepaid successes after cancellation/expiry retain payment truth and create refund work.

Amounts are integer ISO minor units. Taxes are exclusive, configured in basis points (default zero); service charge is fixed minor units (default zero). Each tax is rounded half-up once on the subtotal using integer arithmetic. Delivery fee is zero because delivery is disabled. Quotes last five minutes and bind publication revision, cart version, business-settings version, guest/account scope and exact breakdown. Any change requires a new quote and explicit confirmation. Order submission is transactional and idempotent; provider calls happen after commit via durable work.

Payment, fulfillment and refund states are independent. Pay-at-pickup is enabled by default once ordering is enabled. Collection is an explicit audited action requiring payment permission; completion requires payment collected and refund resolution. No provider is selected. Online payment is production-disabled. A durable fake provider is available ONLY under NODE_ENV=test for deterministic timeout, webhook and reconciliation tests; it never represents merchant approval or real payment. Provider references are scoped by merchant/tenant, events durably deduplicated, exact amount/currency checked and settled payments never regress. Refund reservations serialize against payment balances; pending/confirmed refunds cannot exceed collected funds. No real charge or refund is initiated by this implementation.

Fulfillment: PENDING → CONFIRMED → PREPARING → READY → COMPLETED. PENDING may be REJECTED or CANCELLED; CONFIRMED may be CANCELLED by authorized staff. Customer cancellation immediately cancels a pending order; after confirmation it records a request for staff review, and no cancellation is allowed after preparation starts. PREPARING requires online payment settled, or permitted pay-at-pickup. Completion requires collection. Refund success never follows automatically from cancellation.

Reservations use default-branch cover capacity, not table assignments. Defaults: capacity 20, duration 60 minutes, buffer 15, slot interval 15, minimum lead 60, maximum advance 30 days, max party 8; pending requests hold capacity for 15 minutes (bounded 5–60). Staff confirmation is required. All overlaps, including different start times and buffers, count towards concurrent covers. A restaurant-row lock serializes capacity decisions and reschedules; a sweep of overlap endpoints measures peak covers. Expired pending holds never count even before cleanup. UTC instants are required; availability returns explicit UTC instants with restaurant-local labels including offset, making repeated DST times distinct and avoiding nonexistent local times. Customer changes are permitted only for pending/confirmed future requests. Staff states additionally include SEATED, COMPLETED, NO_SHOW, DECLINED and CANCELLED.

Suspension/unpublication/disabled entitlements block new carts, quotes, orders and reservations. They preserve history and do not cancel paid orders or confirmed reservations. Verified active members with explicit transaction permissions may continue resolution, collections/refunds and reservation management while suspended. Payment callbacks, reconciliation and the outbox continue independently of publication/entitlements. Platform changes require separate platform administrator assignment, reason and audit.

Order/payment/refund/reservation state changes append history and an outbox event in the same transaction. Workers lease rows with SKIP LOCKED, retry with bounded exponential backoff (8 attempts), persist attempt records and support explicit retry of failed deliveries. SMTP has no exactly-once guarantee: delivery can duplicate if a worker dies after SMTP acceptance and before acknowledgement. Records remain the source of truth; polling is every 5 seconds for staff and customer tracking.

Reports use local calendar date boundaries converted by PostgreSQL to UTC. Operational orders use creation date; completed count/AOV/top items use completion date; collected amounts use payment transaction date; refunds use confirmation date. Cash collections are not profit. Currencies are grouped separately and aggregates are computed independently to avoid join multiplication.
